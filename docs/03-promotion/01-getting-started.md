# Wristo Promotion Starter Guide

This guide will help you quickly understand how to join the Wristo promotion program and earn commission by sharing promotion codes.

---

## 1. Apply to Become a Promoter

1. Register an account on the Wristo platform using your email.  
   Sign up at: https://sso.wristo.io/register

   <img src="https://wristo-wiki.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/wiki20260207184302759.png" alt="image-20260207184300584" style="zoom:63%;" />
2. After registration, send an email to: [support@wristo.io](mailto:support@wristo.io)
3. In your email, please include:

   * That you would like to become a Wristo promoter
   * A short self-introduction
   * Links to your social media accounts (such as TikTok, Instagram, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, Kuaishou, etc. — recommended)

> Tip: Providing real and active social media accounts will help increase your chances of approval.

---

## 2. Platform Review

* The platform will review your application after it is received.
* Review results will be:

  * ✅ Approved  
  * ❌ Not approved

* The result will be sent to you via email.

If your application is not approved, you can update your information according to the feedback and apply again.

---

## 3. Fill in Your Payout Information

Once approved, log in to the promotion dashboard and fill in your payout information:

👉 [https://growth.wristo.io/account/profile](https://growth.wristo.io/account/profile)

Steps:

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to the **Profile** page
3. Fill in your payout method and related information
4. Save and submit

> Please make sure all payout information is accurate to avoid issues with settlement.

## 5. Promote and Drive Orders

### 5.1 Publish Promotional Content

Post Wristo-related content on your social media platforms, for example:

* Watch face recommendations
* Experience sharing
* Unboxing videos
* Daily wear showcases

Platforms include but are not limited to:

* Instagram
* YouTube
* TikTok
* Reddit

### 5.2 Guide Users to Use Your Code

In your content, guide users to:

👉 Order page: [https://www.wristo.io/code](https://www.wristo.io/code)

Remind users to enter **your promotion code** when placing an order.

## 7. Payout & Settlement Rules

* Settlement cycle: once per month

* Settlement schedule:

  * Around the 1st of each month, the previous month’s earnings are calculated
  * Payouts are made before the 10th of the month

* Payout method: according to the payout information you filled in on the Profile page

If there are holidays, the settlement date may be postponed.

---

## 8. Promotion Tips (Advanced)

To improve your earnings, we recommend:

1. Keep publishing content

   * Maintain a consistent posting frequency
   * Build your personal style and brand

2. Share real experiences

   * Show real-life wearing scenarios
   * Share genuine usage feedback

3. Clear call-to-action

   * Explicitly tell users “Use my code to get a better deal”
   * Pin your promotion code in the comments

4. Review data regularly

   * Check your dashboard data regularly
   * Adjust your content strategy based on performance

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I apply if I don’t have followers?

Yes. However, we recommend building up some content and activity first to increase your approval rate.

### Q2: Can one person have multiple codes?

Please refer to the backend rules. In general, multiple promotion codes are supported.

### Q3: Can I withdraw my earnings?

Yes. Earnings will be settled monthly to the payout account you provided.

### Q4: How long does it take to receive the payout?

In general, payouts will arrive before the 10th of each month.
