# Dashboard Overview

In this section, instead of walking through every button, we will first clarify the two core systems of Wristo:

- Design tool: `https://studio.wristo.io/`
- Merchant system: `https://merchant.wristo.io/`

---

## 1. Design Tool · Wristo Studio (studio.wristo.io)

- **What it is**  
  Wristo Studio is the daily workspace for designers. Here you can:
  - Manage watch face projects and their versions.  
  - Use a visual editor to create designs (layout, styles, data binding, etc.).  
  - Upload and manage images, fonts, and other design assets.  
  - Preview and export configurations in preparation for publishing.

- **Who is it for?**  
  - Designers who are directly involved in creating and producing watch faces.  
  - Creators who want fine-grained control over visual details and interactions.

- **Typical workflow**  
  1. Log in to `studio.wristo.io`.  
  2. Create or open a watch face project.  
  3. Complete design and data configuration in the editor.  
  4. Preview and export the configuration and asset bundle for publishing.

---

## 2. Merchant System · Wristo Merchant (merchant.wristo.io)

- **What it is**  
  Wristo Merchant is the operations and analytics platform for designers and merchants. Here you can:
  - View downloads and usage data for your watch faces.  
  - View and manage revenue-related data (where supported by the platform).  
  - Manage information for published watch faces (name, description, price, version, etc.).

- **Who is it for?**  
  - Designers who already have live watch faces and want to track performance and revenue.  
  - Merchants / brands responsible for overall operations and monetization.

- **Typical workflow**  
  1. Log in to `merchant.wristo.io`.  
  2. In the watch face list, review key metrics such as downloads and retention for each watch face.  
  3. Adjust pricing, descriptions, and publishing strategies based on data performance.  
  4. Regularly review payout reports and related statements (subject to what the platform supports).

---

> Summary:
> - `studio.wristo.io`: the "design workspace" where you create and configure watch faces.  
> - `merchant.wristo.io`: the "merchant console" where you track data, revenue, and operational performance.  
> We recommend finishing your watch face in Studio first, then using data from Merchant to guide ongoing iterations and business decisions.
