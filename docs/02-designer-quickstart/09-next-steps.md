# Next Steps

- **Publishing to a platform (using Garmin as an example)**  
  - Follow the platform’s process to upload your asset bundle and configuration files.  
  - Fill in watch face name, description, tags, preview images, and other details.  
  - Submit for review and wait for the platform’s result.

- **Updating watch faces and version management**  
  - For each update, it’s recommended to:
    - Keep historical versions in Wristo (or use version numbers to manage them).  
    - Update the version number and changelog in your configuration files.  
  - Avoid overwriting the live version without a backup so you can roll back if necessary.

- **User feedback and iteration**  
  - Adjust your design based on user feedback and metrics (downloads, retention, etc.).  
  - Iterate in small steps: change only a few key elements at a time and keep observing the impact.

- **Monetization paths (if available)**  
  - If the platform supports paid watch faces or in-app purchases:
    - Pay attention to pricing strategy and target audience.  
    - Balance unique, expressive design with mainstream aesthetics.  
  - For specific rules and revenue sharing details, refer to the "Designer Onboarding Rules" and "Designer Onboarding Plan" documents.
