# Getting Started for Designers

- **What is Wristo?**  
  Wristo is a "watch face design and distribution platform" for designers and developers. It helps you quickly turn your ideas into watch faces that run on major smartwatch platforms (such as Garmin).

- **What can I do with Wristo?**  
  - Design and manage multiple watch face projects.  
  - Use a visual editor to handle layout, colors, typography, and other design work.  
  - Bind data fields (steps, heart rate, etc.) to create watch faces that are both beautiful and useful.  
  - Export and publish to supported platforms, and continue maintaining and updating your work.

- **Which devices and platforms are supported? (examples)**  
  - Garmin devices (with different resolutions and models).  
  - More platforms may be added over time depending on support (refer to the latest docs).

- **Who is Wristo for?**  
  - Designers: people with UI/visual design experience who want to quickly create publishable watch faces.  
  - Developers: people who need to customize visuals and interactions on existing watch faces.  
  - Merchants / brands: teams that want to build branded watch face assets.
