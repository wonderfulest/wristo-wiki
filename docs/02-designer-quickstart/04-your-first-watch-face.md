# Your First Watch Face

> This is the most important section in the quickstart. It’s recommended that you follow it step by step.

- **Create your first watch face**  
  - In the console, click "New watch face" or a similar entry point.  
  - Give the project a name (for example: `My First Wristo Watchface`).

- **Choose device and resolution**  
  - Select a target device from the list of supported devices (such as a specific Garmin model).  
  - The corresponding resolution will be filled in automatically, and you can configure multi-resolution support later if needed.

- **Use a template vs. start from scratch**  
  - Using a template:
    - Great for first-time users to quickly understand layout and components.  
    - Choose a template close to your desired style and simply replace images and colors.  
  - Starting from scratch:
    - Suitable for users with a mature design plan or strong design experience.  
    - Plan your own grid, layout, and information hierarchy.

- **Save and preview**  
  - After completing the basic design in the editor, click "Save".  
  - Use the built-in preview to see how the watch face looks on the target device.  
  - It’s recommended to preview after every key change to avoid rework later.
